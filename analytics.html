AUDIT
CURRENT STRATEGY

What I would change first

  1. Stop treating every technology topic equally. Build 3–5 repeatable pillars around practical AI, creator tools, mobile/PC productivity, Nepal-specific digital problems and carefully selected earning topics.
  2. Turn winners into series. One successful topic should produce a long video, multiple Shorts, follow-up comparisons and updated versions when the product changes.
  3. Make packaging a separate job. Every upload should receive an SEO score, search-intent analysis, CTR-oriented title options and a thumbnail concept before production.
  4. Use Shorts as discovery. Shorts should have their own hook and pacing, but connect to a relevant long-form topic when appropriate.
  5. Do not simply translate Nepal content for US/global. Research those markets separately. The research target is different even though your requested script language remains Nepali.
